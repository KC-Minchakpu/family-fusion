Workshop Sessions

Each session is designed to give you practical tools you can use immediately in your home.

Session 1: Communication & Connection

Focus: Strengthening your relationship through better communication

  • How to listen effectively
  • How to express your feelings without causing conflict
  • Simple ways to stay connected despite a busy schedule

Session 2: Managing Conflict & Stress

Focus: Handling disagreements without damaging your relationship

  • How to stay calm during arguments
  • The “Pause and Return” method
  • Practical stress-management tools for daily life

Session 3: Parenting as a Team

Focus: Raising children with consistency and unity

  • Positive discipline strategies
  • How to agree on parenting rules
  • How to support each other as parents

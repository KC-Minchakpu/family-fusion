About the Workshop

Are you a young parent in Abuja trying to balance work, marriage, and raising children?

Our workshop helps you:

  • Communicate better with your spouse
  • Handle conflict calmly and effectively
  • Work together as a parenting team

This is a practical, supportive, and judgment-free program created specifically for families with children ages 0–10.

You don’t need to have problems to attend—this workshop is for anyone who wants a stronger, happier home.
